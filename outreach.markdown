# Outreach

## Mini-workshop on emergence

During my Bachelor's and Master's, I had the opportunity to study the philosophical notion of emergence under various angles. Nowadays, most of my colleagues, who are physicists, biologists, or psychologists by training, investigate emergent phenomena as part of their daily work. So I put together a small workshop to get them acquainted with the philosophical debates on this notion. You can find the slides [here](pdfs/PhilosophyEmergence1.pdf) and [here](pdfs/PhilosophyEmergence2.pdf).
