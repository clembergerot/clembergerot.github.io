# About

Currently, I am a PhD candidate at Humboldt-University Berlin, where I conduct my research under the supervision of Pawel Romanczuk ([Romanczuk Lab](http://lab.romanczuk.de/)) and Wolfram Barfuss ([Barfuss Lab](https://barfusslab.github.io/), University of Bonn). My work aims at better understanding the influence of cognitive heuristics, such as optimism and confirmation biases, on multi-agent learning dynamics. To do so, I use tools from *reinforcement learning*, *agent-based modeling*, *complex systems*, and *game theory*. Between 2021 and 2024, this research was supported by a fellowship from the [Einstein Center for the Neurosciences](https://www.ecn-berlin.de/home.html). 

Previously, I obtained a Master's Degree in Cognitive Science (Cogmaster) from the Ecole des Hautes Etudes en Sciences Sociales in Paris. My Master's thesis, supervised by [Valérian Chambon](https://sites.google.com/site/chambonvalerian/home), touched upon the choice-confirmation bias in individual decision-making. I also hold a Bachelor's Degree in Philosophy from the Université Paris Sciences et Lettres, where I wrote a Bachelor's thesis on *emergence*. To this day, I am still fascinated by emergent phenomena, these unexpected global patterns arising from individual components and their local interactions. 

Another strong interest of mine lies in the notions of *agency* and *action*. During my Master's, I did an Erasmus internship with [Hans Liljenström](https://neurophil-freewill.org/hans-liljenstrom/) on the notion of *free will*, and on the question whether it is possible to (mathematically/computationally) model it. Of course, I did not get all the answers; but these topics have kept stimulating my thoughts and I ended up combining my interests in emergent phenomena and action by studying collective learning and decision-making.

Therefore, my primary expertise lies in *collective (reinforcement) learning*, *decision-making heuristics* and *bounded rationality*. But anything that involves emergent social phenomena will spark my interest -- I feel particularly engaged by research that touches upon *agency*, *autonomy*, *collective action* and *self-governance*, and I welcome any conversation on these topics.
